Who We Are

About NovaRobotics

We are a Botswana-based robotics innovation company building useful digital solutions for businesses, students, startups and organisations.

Our Story

Built for Botswana’s Digital Future

NovaRobotics was created to help local businesses and individuals use technology in a simple and practical way. The company focuses on real problems such as poor online visibility, manual reporting, weak branding and slow business processes.

Our work combines web development, data analytics, business intelligence,Artificial intelligence intergration, system development and design. This allows us to support clients from idea stage up to final digital delivery.
